# Server contour reduction — September 25, 2026

Local measurements on the captured September 24 MRMS/TDWR fixtures. These are
desktop measurements, not physical-device qualification or future test results.

The server retains D3 marching-squares crossings, indexes exterior bounds for
hole assignment, and omits zero-area rings. MRMS then removes collinear points
and simplifies with a maximum deviation of 0.1 native grid cell. A shared edge
index includes all thresholds: replacements cannot cross other edges or sweep
over another contour vertex. Small rings and holes remain. TDWR retains its
polar crossings through geographic projection; the Cartesian tolerance does not
apply to terminal scans.

`node --import=tsx tools/benchmark-radar.ts` compares the pinned D3 implementation
with this pipeline on the same decoded fields. One local run:

| Capture | Before positions | After positions | Before contour/project/serialize | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MRMS CONUS | 679,527 | 420,289 | 5,144 ms | 2,625 ms |
| TOKC | 45,452 | 41,794 | 135 ms | 30 ms |
| TATL | 7,944 | 7,136 | 16 ms | 13 ms |

MRMS decoding took 390 ms in both paths. Optimized contour extraction took
2,037 ms and simplification 523 ms. Geometry JSON declined from 12,766,197 to
7,943,392 bytes (38%); these counts exclude the small feature/provenance wrapper.
Removing only zero-area rings and redundant straight/repeated positions leaves
587,821 positions (14% fewer). The remaining reduction uses the bounded tolerance;
it is not mathematically lossless.

Chromium 153.0.8010.12 in the pinned Playwright 1.63 container, software rendering,
1024×768 at DPR 1, centered at −97°, 37°. Both paths used the existing individual
polygon features, eight threshold layers, buffer 8, tolerance 0.2, opacity 0.75.
Data was loaded before timing. Three alternating runs with fresh sources, while
the separate Chromium regression suite was also running:

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 634 ms | 451 ms | 29% |

No map errors. Visual inspection retained the same echoes and threshold structure.
Of 786,432 screenshot pixels, 442 changed at zoom 4 and 609 at zoom 7; 86 and 82
respectively changed by more than 32 on any RGB channel. Small edge differences
are expected from the bounded tolerance and MapLibre's own tile simplification.
These captures do not exhaust every storm geometry, zoom or device.

`test/weather-radar-contours.test.ts` compares indexed contour rings and hole
ownership against D3 on deterministic fields, including across index buckets.
Exact-threshold plateaus can expose a D3 containment defect: a repeated vertex
can claim a distant point on the same row. The implementation checks both
coordinates and the suite retains a focused regression for correct hole ownership.
It also checks the displacement bound,
and protects tiny echoes, holes and adjacent thresholds from simplification.
Captured radar decoding and the existing rendering regressions remain required.
